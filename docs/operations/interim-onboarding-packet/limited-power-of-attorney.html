Limited Power of Attorney

This Limited Power of Attorney is made effective as of ___________________, by _________________________________, MC ____________ (CARRIER), in favour of Iron Wing Logistics L.L.C. (DISPATCH).

CARRIER appoints DISPATCH as its attorney-in-fact (AGENT) only for the following purposes, in connection with loads DISPATCH arranges for CARRIER under the Dispatch Service Agreement:

  1. Contact shippers and brokers to find cargo on behalf of CARRIER.
  2. Contact CARRIER's insurance provider to obtain certificates of insurance listing a broker or shipper as certificate holder.
  3. Send paperwork (carrier packets, rate confirmations, certificates of insurance, invoices, and other necessary load paperwork) to shippers and brokers on behalf of CARRIER.
  4. Sign carrier setup packets on behalf of CARRIER.
  5. Sign rate confirmations and other load documents on behalf of CARRIER, only for loads CARRIER has approved.
  6. Give brokers and shippers the remittance (payment) details CARRIER has provided to DISPATCH in writing, without changing them.

This power is limited to the purposes listed above. DISPATCH may not sign or change bank, ACH, remittance or payment instructions on CARRIER's behalf, receive CARRIER's freight payments, or act for CARRIER in any other matter.

DISPATCH shall not be liable for any loss resulting from an error of judgment made in good faith. DISPATCH shall be liable for willful misconduct or failure to act in good faith while acting under this Limited Power of Attorney. CARRIER agrees that any third party may rely on this document when dealing with DISPATCH for the purposes above.

This Limited Power of Attorney takes effect immediately and remains in effect until CARRIER revokes it by email to info@iron-wing-dispatching.com, or until the Dispatch Service Agreement ends, whichever comes first. Revoking it ends the Dispatch Service Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, CARRIER has executed this Limited Power of Attorney on the date below.
