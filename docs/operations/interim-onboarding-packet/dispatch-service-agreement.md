# Dispatch Service Agreement

This Dispatch Service Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of
___________________, by and between **Iron Wing Logistics L.L.C.** (DISPATCH) and
_________________________________, MC ____________, USDOT ____________
(CARRIER).

WHEREAS, DISPATCH is a truck dispatching service that works for the CARRIER,
finding loads and handling the necessary communication and paperwork between a
BROKER or shipper and the CARRIER in order to secure cargo for the CARRIER.
DISPATCH is not a freight broker.

WHEREAS, CARRIER is a licensed motor carrier operating under its own
authority.

DISPATCH and CARRIER, upon agreeing to due consideration, agree to the
following terms and conditions:

## 1. Documents

CARRIER must furnish DISPATCH with the following documents before dispatch
begins, by email to info@iron-wing-dispatching.com:

- ______ This Dispatch Service Agreement, signed
- ______ Copy of CARRIER's operating authority (MC)
- ______ Certificate of Insurance
- ______ Signed W-9 form
- ______ Payment Authorization Form (card, bank debit or both), signed
- ______ Limited Power of Attorney, signed
- ______ Notice of Assignment from CARRIER's factoring company (only if
  CARRIER factors)

## 2. Relationship

The relationship of CARRIER to DISPATCH shall, at all times, be that of an
independent contractor. DISPATCH shall be the agent of CARRIER for searching
for loads, negotiating and booking them, dispatching, and handling load
paperwork directly with the BROKER or shipper, within the limits of the
Limited Power of Attorney.

## 3. Term

This Agreement is effective as of the date above and has **no minimum term**.
Either party may end it at any time by telling the other party by phone or
email. Fees remain due on every load DISPATCH booked before the Agreement
ended, as set out in Section 7.

## 5. Description of services

DISPATCH will handle all necessary load-related documentation and
communication with the BROKER by phone, fax or email.

DISPATCH will be listed as the primary contact on load-related documentation,
including carrier setup packets and rate confirmations. Under the Limited
Power of Attorney, DISPATCH may sign carrier setup packets and rate
confirmations for loads CARRIER has approved.

DISPATCH will be the primary point of contact for obtaining certificates of
insurance listing a BROKER as certificate holder.

DISPATCH will search for, locate and book loads that match CARRIER's profile,
including its equipment, lanes, freight CARRIER will not haul, and the lowest
rate CARRIER will accept. DISPATCH may post CARRIER's equipment on load
boards. DISPATCH will check a BROKER's credit and payment record before
booking and will negotiate rates on CARRIER's behalf to the best of its
ability. **DISPATCH will present each load to CARRIER for approval before
making a binding commitment, and CARRIER may turn down any load.** Once a load
is approved, DISPATCH will send the necessary documents to the BROKER.

When necessary and within its capabilities, DISPATCH will assist CARRIER with
issues that arise during transit, and will follow up with a BROKER who pays
CARRIER late or short, without guaranteeing an outcome.

After delivery of a load arranged by DISPATCH, DISPATCH will send all related
paperwork to the BROKER for billing within 12 hours of receiving it from
CARRIER.

Administrative support included in both plans: carrier packets, bills of
lading, proofs of delivery and rate confirmations. Support is available during
business hours on the Essential Plan.

DISPATCH does not guarantee any level of revenue, number of loads, or rates.

## 6. Carrier obligations

**Fee.** CARRIER agrees to pay DISPATCH the percentage of its plan (Section 4)
of the **linehaul rate** on the final rate confirmation of each load booked by
DISPATCH. CARRIER will not pay DISPATCH any fee on fuel surcharge, detention,
layover, truck-ordered-not-used pay, extra-stop pay, driver-assist pay, or
reimbursements such as lumper fees. DISPATCH may change its published pricing
for future loads by giving CARRIER 7 days' notice by email; a change never
affects loads already booked.

CARRIER agrees to deliver each tendered shipment to its designated
destination, free of damage or loss.

CARRIER agrees to email DISPATCH all signed bills of lading (BOL) or proofs of
delivery (POD), and any additional documents such as receipts and photos
associated with a load arranged by DISPATCH, within 12 hours of receiving
them.

CARRIER agrees to maintain all licenses and permits required by the USDOT and
FMCSA and to comply with all State and Federal regulations for its area of
operation, including, but not limited to, California CARB compliance,
including changes to those laws.

CARRIER agrees to maintain insurance of at least $1,000,000 in liability
coverage and at least $100,000 in cargo coverage. CARRIER shall have the
sole and exclusive care, custody and control of the shipper's property from
pickup until delivery, and assumes the liability of a motor carrier for loss,
delay, damage to or destruction of the shipper's goods while in its care.

CARRIER agrees to indemnify DISPATCH and hold DISPATCH harmless for any damage
to persons or property arising from CARRIER performing its duties.

CARRIER agrees never to turn down a load presented by DISPATCH and then
contact the BROKER directly to accept the same load in order to avoid paying
the DISPATCH fee.

DISPATCH will take no action to ensure CARRIER's compliance, and CARRIER
agrees to indemnify DISPATCH against any violation of applicable law by
CARRIER. CARRIER agrees to provide, operate and maintain in good working
condition the vehicles and equipment needed to transport freight safely, and
to provide properly qualified, trained and licensed drivers. CARRIER's
personnel shall conduct themselves professionally and comply with each
facility's rules while on its premises.

CARRIER agrees that all freight tendered to it through DISPATCH shall be
transported on equipment operated only under CARRIER's authority, and that
CARRIER shall not sub-contract, broker or otherwise arrange for the freight to
be transported by a third party without the prior written consent of
DISPATCH.

## 7. Compensation

**Weekly charge.** The amount due to DISPATCH will be charged automatically
using the payment method or methods CARRIER provides on the Payment
Authorization Form: a debit or credit card, a bank debit (ACH) from CARRIER's
business bank account, or both. If CARRIER provides both, CARRIER chooses
which is charged first, and DISPATCH charges the other only if the first is
declined or returned. **Every Friday**, DISPATCH will charge the fees on all
loads delivered during the previous week (Monday to Sunday), and on loads
CARRIER cancelled during that week, and will email CARRIER a statement listing
each load, its linehaul rate and its fee. A bank debit may take one to three
business days to settle. This applies whether or not CARRIER uses a factoring
company.

**Fee owed on delivery.** The fee for a load is owed once the load is
delivered. It remains owed if the BROKER or shipper pays late, pays short, or
does not pay.

**Cancelled loads.** If CARRIER cancels a load after accepting it, for any
reason (including, but not limited to, breakdown, hours of service, late
arrival or unloading delays), the fee on that load is still owed, on the
linehaul rate of its rate confirmation, and is charged on the Friday after the
cancellation. If the BROKER or shipper cancels a load, no fee is charged on
it; if a fee for that load was already charged, it is credited on the next
charge.

**Questions about a charge.** CARRIER should raise any question about a
statement within 14 days by email or phone. DISPATCH will correct any error
by refund or by credit on the next charge.

**Declined charge.** If a charge is declined or a bank debit is returned,
DISPATCH will tell CARRIER and retry within 3 business days, using the backup
method if CARRIER provided one. If the fees are still unpaid, DISPATCH may stop
booking new loads until they are paid. Loads already booked are not affected.

**After cancelling.** Fees on loads booked before this Agreement ended are
charged on the Friday after each load is delivered or cancelled by CARRIER.

CARRIER will be paid directly by the BROKER or shipper handling the load, or
by a factoring company chosen by CARRIER. CARRIER's freight payments never
pass through DISPATCH.

## 8. Other provisions

DISPATCH bears no financial or legal responsibility in transactions between
the BROKER and CARRIER and will not be liable for any incidental,
consequential or indirect damages, loss of profits, or business interruption
arising out of the use of the service. DISPATCH's total liability for any
claim will not exceed the fees CARRIER paid in the three months before the
claim arose. CARRIER agrees to hold DISPATCH harmless, during and after this
Agreement, from all direct or indirect damages resulting from CARRIER's
hauling of shipper's freight. This includes, but is not limited to, loading
problems, delays, overages, shortages, damages, billing and collection issues,
and hours of service (HOS). CARRIER agrees to operate under its own motor
carrier authority, is responsible for complying with all USDOT and FMCSA
regulations, and will notify DISPATCH of changes to its authority, insurance,
carrier profile or ownership.

## 9. Accessorial charges

On the Full-Service Plan, DISPATCH will attempt in good faith to collect
accessorial charges, such as detention, layover and lumper reimbursement, as
per BROKER policies, with no guarantee of collection. On the Essential Plan,
CARRIER pursues its own accessorial charges. DISPATCH is not responsible for
accessorial charges that remain uncollected, and CARRIER agrees to hold
DISPATCH harmless for any uncollected charges. DISPATCH takes no fee on
accessorial pay (Section 6).

## 10. Additional provisions

CARRIER agrees to notify DISPATCH on the day the truck or trucks are available
for dispatch and to advise DISPATCH of any status changes or delays
immediately.

Each party will keep the other's business information (rates, customers,
documents and account details) confidential and use it only for this
Agreement.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Wyoming, without
regard to conflict-of-law rules.

This Agreement, the Limited Power of Attorney and the Payment Authorization Form
are the whole agreement between the parties about dispatch services.

## Schedule A: Full-Service Plan services

To the extent possible, on the Full-Service Plan DISPATCH agrees to:

- Provide priority support 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Assign CARRIER a dedicated dispatcher who knows CARRIER's lanes, trailer and
  goals.
- Prepare CARRIER's invoices, keep receipts, and prepare IFTA reports for the
  loads arranged by DISPATCH.
- Keep CARRIER's load documents for loads arranged by DISPATCH on file (rate
  confirmation, bill of lading, receipts and photos if any, and invoices) and
  provide them to CARRIER within 12 hours of a request.
- Assist CARRIER with factoring.
- Pursue lumper and detention claims as set out in Section 9.
- Assist CARRIER with compliance.
