# Dispatcher closing and onboarding checklist

## After the call

- [ ] Record Jack/source call reference and closing dispatcher; confirm this was a service agreement discussion, not simply a callback.
- [ ] Confirm carrier legal name/DBA, MC and USDOT; resolve identity conflicts with the carrier.
- [ ] Confirm signer name/title and authority to sign; read back the recipient email.
- [ ] Confirm Essential 5%, Full-Service 10%, or the exact agreed fleet percentage and service scope. Select one plan consistently in both agreement and authorization. Do not infer fleet pricing.
- [ ] Confirm effective/service start date. For current billing, earliest eligibility is November 23, 2026; agree a later carrier-specific date where appropriate.
- [ ] Explain linehaul-only fees, Friday charging for the previous Monday–Sunday, carrier-cancelled accepted loads, and payment setup through Stripe. Use the agreement for full terms; do not improvise promises.
- [ ] Confirm card, ACH or both, and requested primary/backup. Never request account/card numbers.
- [ ] Record fleet/equipment, lanes, availability, factoring status and operational contact details privately.

## Before sending

- [ ] Use the approved released packet, not the watermarked review copy.
- [ ] Verify company/MC/USDOT, effective date, fee and signer details match across all documents. Sender-confirmed commercial fields must not remain blank or freely editable by the client.
- [ ] Ensure every signature/date/name field is assigned to the correct Carrier or Dispatch recipient.
- [ ] Inspect the full rendered packet including Schedule A. Do not silently remove schedules or rewrite clauses.
- [ ] Check the register/provider for an existing open request. On an uncertain send result, look up the existing request; do not immediately send a duplicate.
- [ ] Explicitly review recipient and authorize sending this packet.
- [ ] Record packet version, provider document ID, sent timestamp and next follow-up date.

## Before dispatch begins

- [ ] Both required parties have signed; download final signed PDF and audit evidence.
- [ ] Obtain and review operating authority, insurance certificate, signed W-9, and factoring Notice of Assignment if applicable. Collect sensitive attachments privately, not in a public form/repository.
- [ ] Complete a carrier profile covering equipment, lanes, minimum rates and excluded freight. Existing business folder has `Carrier Profile Sheet.docx`; it has not been reviewed or converted in this packet.
- [ ] Confirm payment selection matches the signed authorization; verify actual successful Stripe setup/required mandate and bank verification. An emailed link or return-page screenshot is not completion evidence.
- [ ] Create/verify the operational carrier relationship and real fleet assets in Kelevo through the approved manual process. Never use broker/customer or driver onboarding as an assumed client-carrier substitute. Escalate missing model support.
- [ ] Confirm billing carrier ID, fee and eligible date, with actual driver/truck mapping verified before billable work. Payment setup alone does not establish this mapping.
- [ ] Record who verified operational readiness and when. Any incomplete dependency remains blocked with an owner/next action.

Do not build or approve a billing run to test onboarding. Weekly collections remain a separate reviewed operation.
